🏛️ Parliament Operations Context

POZ Requirements Coverage:
  • Weekly Sittings: House of Assembly (270 members) and Senate (80 members)
  • Committee Meetings: Portfolio committee meetings as needed
  • Constituency Visits: Monthly 120L allocation per Member/Senator (350 total)
  • National Events: Independence Day, Heroes Day, Culture Day, Youth Day
  • Annual Events: Zimbabwe Trade Fair, Pre-Budget Seminar
  • Field Visits: Public hearings and constituency field visits
  • Training Programs: Parliamentary training and capacity building
  • Distribution Programs: Fuel coupon distribution activities
