👑 SUPERUSER / ADMIN

Role: Overall system administration and policy management

Parliament Access: Full system access including operations management

  • Parliament Operations (Full Management)
  • Members Management
  • Parliament Sessions
  • Programs
  • Attendance Tracking
  • Fuel Allocations
  • System Analytics
  • Audit & Compliance

Role: PRIMARY Parliament Operations Management

Responsibility: Regional parliament session management and local operations

  • Parliament Operations Management
  • Members Management (Regional)
  • Parliament Sessions (Create/Manage)
  • Programs (Organize)
  • Attendance Tracking
  • Fuel Allocations (Regional)
  • Session Manager Assignment
  • SubCenter Association

Updated Role Hierarchy

  1. SUPERUSER/ADMIN - Overall system administration
  2. SUB_CENTER Officers - PRIMARY Parliament operations management
  3. MAIN_CENTER Officers - Parliament system oversight and coordination
  4. BENEFICIARIES - Individual account management only

🎯 Expected User Experience

When SUB_CENTER logs in:

  • ✅ Sees "PARLIAMENT OPERATIONS" section
  • ✅ Can create and manage parliament sessions
  • ✅ Can assign session managers
  • ✅ Can organize programs and events

When MAIN_CENTER logs in:

  • ✅ Sees "PARLIAMENT OVERSIGHT" section
  • ✅ Can monitor subcenter activities
  • ✅ Can view system-wide parliament reports
  • ❌ No direct parliament operations management

When BENEFICIARY logs in:

  • ✅ Sees "MY ACCOUNT" section only
  • ✅ Can view personal allocations and transactions
  • ❌ No parliament operations access
  • ❌ No system management capabilities
