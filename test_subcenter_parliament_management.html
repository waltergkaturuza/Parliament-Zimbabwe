🎯 Implementation Overview

The Parliament of Zimbabwe Fuel Coupon System has been enhanced to allow SUB_CENTER officers to manage parliament operations in their regions. This decentralizes parliament management while maintaining system oversight.

🔗 New Management Hierarchy

1. SUPERUSER/ADMIN - Overall system administration and policy management
2. SUB_CENTER Officers - PRIMARY Parliament Operations Management
  • Create and manage parliament sessions for their region
  • Assign themselves or other officers as session managers
  • Track MP and staff attendance at sessions
  • Calculate and distribute fuel entitlements
  • Organize local parliament-related events and programs
3. MAIN_CENTER Officers - System oversight and coordination
  • Monitor sub-center operations
  • System-wide reporting and analytics
  • Cross-regional coordination
